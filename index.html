Guidebook

Save a link

Pick up the mallet and choose Start your own Library. Paste a link into your library to keep it on this device.

Explore the room

Pick up a bottle, open a book, or look inside a box. Click the darkened room to put the object back.

Find it again

Every saved link lives in All items. Use collections to keep related links together.

Arrange a shelf

Drag an object onto another to trade places. Drop it on bare wood to place it on that shelf. A drawer can hold it until you bring it back.

Keyboard controls

Press Enter to inspect. Use Alt and an arrow key to shift an object, or H to put it in the Unfiled drawer.
